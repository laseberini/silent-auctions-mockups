Colour themes

The same feed (option 1) in four looks. Each school would choose its own colours; these show how far the theming can go.

1Original
Sunnyside teal header, yellow Bid buttons, neutral page.
2Watermelon, green header
Bright rind-green header with seed-black text, pale-rind page, flesh-red Bid buttons.
3Watermelon on the cards
As 2, and every card is a slice: pale pink panel, pale-rind stripe and green rind along the bottom, red item-number tags.
4Glass
Frosted-glass look in the spirit of Apple's latest design: photos fill each card with the title, price and Bid on a floating glass panel; glass header, pills and sheets over a soft colour field.
